1. INTRODUCTION & WORKFLOW
--------------------------
Mubarak POS is built for high-speed counter service, dine-in table management,
and delivery operations. It runs fully offline with zero internet dependency,
while offering optional automated Google Drive cloud backup.

2. CASHIER DAILY OPERATIONS
---------------------------
A. Opening Shift & Starting Cash:
   1. Log in with your Cashier account (e.g., food_cashier@gmail.com).
   2. When prompted, enter your opening cash float amount in the cash drawer.
   3. The Cashier Dashboard will open.

B. Creating an Order (New Order Screen):
   1. Click "New Order" on the top navigation bar.
   2. (Optional) Select a Table number (e.g. Table 1 to Table 15) for dine-in orders.
   3. (Optional) Assign a Waiter (Tariq, Hamza, or Usman).
   4. Filter items by Category using the horizontal category bar:
      - Parachinari Lobya / Karahi
      - Parachinari Lobya Seekh Karahi
      - Cultural Tikka Seekh / Karahi
      - Parachinari Chawal / Pulao
      - Fast Food / Burgers
      - Parathas
      - Beverages / Chai
   5. Select Products:
      - Click any product card.
      - For items with portions (e.g. Parachinari Lobya Karahi), a portion
        selection modal appears with options:
        * Quarter (Rs. 400)
        * 2 Quarter (Rs. 550)
        * 3 Quarter (Rs. 700)
        * 4 Quarter (Rs. 850)
      - For Cultural Tikka Seekh items:
        * Half Dozen (Rs. 400 or Rs. 550)
        * Full Dozen (Rs. 800 or Rs. 1,100)
      - Click your preferred portion to add it immediately to the Current Bill.
   6. Search:
      - Use the quick search field to type food names or codes (e.g. "Paratha", "Lobya").

C. Current Bill & Modifications:
   - Increase or decrease item quantities using the + / - buttons.
   - Add kitchen special instructions or item notes.
   - View automatic subtotals, tax calculations, and discount deductions.

D. Draft Orders:
   - If a customer needs time to decide, click "Hold / Save Draft".
   - Retrieve held orders at any time from the "Draft Orders" tab.

E. Payment & Settlement:
   1. Click "Pay" (or press Enter).
   2. Choose Payment Method: Cash, Credit Card, or Mobile QR.
   3. Enter amount tendered. The system calculates change to return.
   4. Click "Complete Payment".
   5. The bill is assigned a unique sequential order number (e.g. MCR-00104).
   6. An 80mm thermal receipt and kitchen order ticket (KOT) are generated.

3. PRINTER SETUP & HARDWARE CONFIGURATION
-----------------------------------------
A. Supported Hardware:
   - Any standard 80mm or 58mm Windows ESC/POS thermal receipt printer
     (USB, Network/Ethernet, or Bluetooth).
   - Cash drawers connected via RJ11/RJ12 cable to the receipt printer.

B. Printer Assignment:
   1. Log in as Administrator (food_admin@gmail.com).
   2. Navigate to "Settings" -> "Printers".
   3. You will find two pre-configured profiles:
      - Cashier Receipt Printer (80mm width, Auto Cut enabled)
      - Kitchen Ticket Printer (80mm width)
   4. In the "Windows Printer" dropdown, select your installed thermal printer
      (e.g., POS-80, Epson TM-T20, Xprinter, Rongta).
   5. Click "Save Printer Configuration" and "Print Test Page".

C. PDF Fallback Printing:
   - If no physical thermal printer is connected, Mubarak POS automatically
     generates an 80mm formatted PDF receipt and saves it to:
     C:\RestaurantPOS\receipts\
   - The PDF can be opened, previewed, or emailed at any time.

4. INVENTORY & STOCK MANAGEMENT
-------------------------------
1. Navigate to the "Inventory" tab on the Administrator dashboard.
2. View current stock levels, ingredient tracking, and low-stock alerts.
3. Record incoming raw supplies, supplier payments, and culinary wastage.

5. REPORTS & FINANCIAL ANALYTICS
--------------------------------
1. Navigate to "Reports".
2. Select desired date ranges (Today, This Week, This Month, or Custom Period).
3. View:
   - Total Gross & Net Sales
   - Sales by Category & Top Performing Dishes
   - Tax Summaries
   - Cash Drawer & Shift Reconciliation
4. Click "Export to Excel" (.xlsx) or "Export to PDF" to generate printable audit files.
   Saved to: C:\RestaurantPOS\reports\

6. AUTOMATED & CLOUD BACKUP
---------------------------
A. Automatic Local Backups:
   - The system automatically triggers an SQLite WAL checkpoint and backup to:
     C:\RestaurantPOS\backups\
   - Backups are timestamped and preserved across sessions.

B. Google Drive Cloud Sync:
   - Configured with official restaurant backup folder:
     Google Drive Folder ID: 1v6t9jR1w3caFNgbICpkylTZd4ZZetkmH
   - In Settings -> Backup, click "Authorize Google Drive" to sync backups to the cloud.

7. END OF DAY PROCEDURES
------------------------
1. Close all open draft orders.
2. Under "Shift Management", click "End Shift / Cash Count".
3. Enter physical drawer count.
4. Print the "Z-Report" (End of Day financial summary).
5. Log out.
